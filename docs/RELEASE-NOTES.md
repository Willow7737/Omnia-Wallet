## What's new

### A new look

The visual language is rebuilt on the design tokens Bluesky ships
(`@bsky.app/alf@0.1.15`), transcribed from source rather than eyeballed.
`docs/DESIGN.md` records where every number came from.

- **Three themes** — Light, Dim (the default dark, a soft blue-grey) and Dark
  (true black for OLED), pickable in Settings. Dark themes are generated by
  mirroring the light ramps, so the two cannot drift apart.
- **Flat surfaces** — no elevation, no ink ripples, hairline dividers instead of
  filled cards, pill-shaped buttons at every size.
- **Iconsax throughout**, replacing Material Icons. Each glyph ships as a
  linear/bold pair, which is exactly what the tab bar's active state needs.
- **Bottom sheets and a top toast** replace every `AlertDialog`, `PopupMenuButton`
  and `SnackBar`.
- **Press feedback** is scale-and-opacity rather than a ripple, and the haptic
  fires on touch-*down* — perceived latency tracks when the feedback lands, not
  when the navigation does.
- **Haptics** are rate-limited so a settling list doesn't buzz, and clamped on
  Android, whose medium impact is far heavier than the iOS equivalent. A
  Settings switch turns them off entirely.
- **Five-tab shell** — Home, Activity, News, Notifications, Profile — each tab
  keeping its own navigation stack and scroll position.

### Conversations

Replies were a flat list one level deep. They are now a real thread.

- **Unlimited nesting**, with the connecting rails and the quarter-circle elbow
  that make a reply read as an answer rather than a new comment that happens to
  be indented.
- **Long threads fold.** A comment's answers stay behind **Show replies** until
  asked for, one level at a time — the stacked faces of who is in there, and a
  chevron badge saying the row opens something.
- **A rule between comments, and only between comments.** Each top-level comment
  is a separate conversation; a line between a comment and its own replies would
  contradict what the connectors say.
- **Omnia's posts carry a verified tick.** Posts only, deliberately — see
  *Security notes* below.

### Reactions

- Likes and dislikes on posts and replies, one table serving both.
- The finger drives the screen and the network follows: a tap registers
  immediately, a burst of taps sends one request carrying the final state, and a
  reply landing from elsewhere never overwrites what you just did.
- Realtime, so a like landing on another device updates an open feed.

### Media

- **Photos open full screen** — hero flight, pinch and double-tap zoom, drag to
  dismiss.
- **Video** in posts and replies: tap to play, scrub, mute, pauses when the app
  leaves the foreground, no autoplay. Tapping the picture opens it full screen
  the way a photo does; the play button plays in place for a look without losing
  your place. Landscape is allowed while a clip is full screen and portrait is
  restored on exit.
- **Link previews** — a post pointing somewhere shows a card for it, *as well as*
  its own attachment rather than instead of it.
- **Share a post as a picture**, painted on a canvas so it is readable wherever
  it lands, with the app link as share text.

### Navigation and lists

- **Activity is in the right order.** Today's transfers were appearing at the
  bottom, under last week's, and Home's "recent activity" was showing the six
  *oldest* entries.
- **Notifications open their subject** — a send notification opens that transfer,
  a news notification that post — instead of dropping you on the tab.
- **Back to top** appears when you are deep in a list and scrolling upward,
  measured in screenfuls rather than a fixed pixel count.

### Sign-in

- **You no longer have to tap twice.** The screen stays in a waiting state with a
  spinner and a way to cancel, until the deep link returns, the app resumes
  holding a session, or 90 seconds pass. It previously cleared the moment the
  browser opened, so coming back looked like nothing had happened.
- **The browser stops asking to open Omnia.** The OAuth screen now opens in a
  Custom Tab belonging to this app rather than the standalone browser, which
  could not close itself on a custom-scheme redirect and reopened the callback
  on every later launch.
- **Sessions survive concurrent use.** Two token refreshes at once meant the
  second presented a token the first had already spent, and the user was signed
  out for no visible reason. Refreshes are now shared.

## Fixes

- Rapid tapping a like could show 2 likes from one person.
- A connecting line ran from one comment, past its own reply, into the next
  unrelated comment.
- Past the maximum indent, a reply's elbow hooked out to the right and back
  through its own avatar.
- Thread rails were drawn dashed — a gap at every row boundary, plus a notch
  where a passing rail met a child's elbow. Both now run unbroken.
- The "Show replies" row sat out of column with the replies it stands for.
- A double yellow underline under toast text.
- The notifications empty state sat in the upper third of the screen instead of
  centred.
- The post page visibly slid before the media viewer appeared.
- The heart is red, not magenta.
- The overflow (⋯) button sat mid-row instead of at the trailing edge.
- The share card drew a hand-drawn ring that read as a stray "O" instead of the
  Omnia mark.
- The share card no longer downloads an entire video only to fail to decode it.
- Blank SVGs across the app.
- Android release builds failed resolving a second Android toolchain that
  `share_plus` pulls in.

## Under the hood

- Threading is three separable pieces — which rows exist, where every line goes,
  and the painting — because connector bugs are silent: a rail running to the
  wrong place lays out and paints without a warning. The first two are pure
  functions with their own tests.
- **262 tests**, up from the low 200s, including measurements and pixel
  assertions for the things that render without complaint when wrong.
- `docs/DESIGN.md` documents the design system and the provenance of each token.
- `pubspec.lock` is pinned to Flutter 3.35 / Dart 3.9 to match the target
  machine. Resolving with a newer toolchain raises the lock's SDK floor above it.

## Deploying this release

Three things live outside the app. **The first two are required** — the release
misbehaves without them.

1. **Apply the reactions migrations** if not already applied:
   `supabase/migrations/20260727000000_news_reactions.sql` and
   `20260727000100_news_reactions_tuning.sql`. Without them, reacting fails.

2. **Set `OMNIA_JWT_SECRET`** in the Supabase Edge Function secrets to exactly
   the value the node verifies with. A mismatch makes the node answer
   `invalid token: invalid signature`, which the app now surfaces verbatim
   instead of the misleading "Session expired" it used to show. Signing in again
   can never fix it. Watch for a trailing newline when copying the value.

3. **Google consent screen branding** (cosmetic, but visible to every new user).
   It currently shows the Supabase project host rather than "Omnia". Fix it in
   the Google Cloud console under *APIs & Services → OAuth consent screen* by
   setting the app name and a verified domain — it is not an app-side setting.

## Known limitations

- **The Android release build has not been verified for this release.** The
  toolchain fix for `share_plus` is in, but there is no Android SDK in the
  environment these changes were made in, so `flutter build apk` has not been
  run against them. Build before you ship.
- Reply authors are not verified and cannot be — see below.

## Security notes

- **The verified tick applies to posts only.** `news_posts` has no INSERT policy,
  so under RLS its author is written by the team and can be trusted. A reply's
  `author_name` is free text from whoever is signed in, so verifying replies by
  name would hand the tick to anyone who set their display name to "omnia".
  There is a test asserting that a reply calling itself omnia gets nothing.
- Reaction policies are scoped to the acting user, with the counts view running
  `security_invoker` so RLS is not bypassed by reading through it.
